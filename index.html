    Compliance

    The Labour (Amendment) Act 2026, and what it changes on Monday morning

    The amendment took effect on 10 April 2026, following the 2025 Ordinance, and touches roughly ninety points of the Labour Act 2006. I studied it because an HR department that misses these dates gets found out at the next buyer audit. Here is the short version I work from.

    01 · Coverage

    Who counts as a worker

    Coverage now follows the nature of the work rather than the job title, so classifications have to be checked against what people actually do. Managerial, administrative and supervisory roles stay outside the definition.

    02 · Unions

    Registration and dues

    Union registration uses fixed member numbers scaled to the size of the establishment, with a cap of three registered unions per establishment. Dues deduction applies to all registered unions and must be deposited within fifteen days.

    03 · Conduct

    Anti-union practice

    Blacklisting, employer-controlled unions and retaliation against complainants are covered. The Director General of Labour can order these stopped and award compensation without going through the Labour Court.

    04 · Benefits

    Leave and end of service

    Maternity leave, festival holidays, lay-off eligibility, death compensation, dismissal and resignation benefits, the wage revision cycle and eviction notice periods all changed — which means payroll configuration, not just the policy document.

    05 · Dignity

    Harassment and safety

    New prohibitions cover forced labour, gender-based violence and sexual harassment. Every establishment must form a five-member complaint committee chaired by a woman with a female majority; fifty or more workers also requires a safety committee, and workers may refuse dangerous work.

    06 · Exposure

    Penalties

    Fines have risen substantially and imprisonment applies to more serious violations, including those causing injury or death. The Alternative Dispute Resolution Authority exists in law but is not operational yet, so disputes still go to the Labour Court.

    First 30 days
    • Reclassify the workforce against actual duties
    • Constitute the complaint committee
    • Update leave configuration in the HR system, not only in the policy text
    By 60 days
    • Recalculate end-of-service reserves
    • Put an audit trail on union dues deduction
    • Revise contract templates and the employee handbook
    • Establish or verify the safety committee
    By 90 days
    • Train line managers on retaliation, interference and blacklisting
    • Build a grievance route people will actually use
    • Prepare the file for buyer and supplier audits

    Summarised from a published HR briefing on the amendment (MiHCM, 2026). Implementing rules for the harassment framework are still pending, and figures and section references should be confirmed against the gazetted text before anyone acts on them.

    Her world

    What I carry home from work

    People tell you to keep a CV neutral. But the way a person spends a quiet evening usually explains how they behave on a difficult Tuesday, so here is mine.

    Old songs, mostly

    The ones recorded long before I was born. There is a patience in them that newer music keeps trying to skip, and I think it shows up in how I listen to people on a phone screening.

    The riverbank

    My favourite place to think is wherever the water is wide and nobody is in a hurry. Most of what I have decided properly, I decided sitting beside a river first.

    Growing things

    Plants only reward people who show up regularly and notice small changes early. That is also the entire job description of HR operations, so I consider it practice.

    Looking up

    I take more photographs of the sky than of anything else. A good evening sky resets a long day faster than anything I have found, and it costs nothing.

    Coffee, properly made

    The first cup of the day is not negotiable, and I will happily take the long way to a good one. Most of my best filing systems were designed while a cup went cold beside me.

    A very small guitar

    I play the ukulele — badly enough to stay humble, well enough to keep going. Four strings turn out to be plenty when the point is the evening, not the performance.
